# User Guide

This guide walks you through every feature of the Cookest app as an end user — from creating your account to cooking your first AI-planned meal.

## What you can do with Cookest

- **Plan your week** — Generate an AI-scored 7-day meal plan tailored to your taste preferences, dietary restrictions, and household size
- **Browse recipes** — Search and filter a curated recipe library; bookmark favourites and filter by what's already in your pantry
- **Manage your pantry** — Track what you have, get alerts before items expire, and let the app deduct ingredients automatically when you cook
- **Build a shopping list** — Auto-generate a grocery list from your meal plan, compare prices across local supermarkets (Pro), and check items off as you shop
- **Cook step-by-step** — Follow recipes in a distraction-free cooking mode with a step counter, timers, and an AI cooking assistant
- **Get AI help** — Ask the built-in AI assistant anything about cooking — substitutions, timing, techniques — contextualised to your current recipe
